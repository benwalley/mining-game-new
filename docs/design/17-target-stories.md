## 17. Target stories

These stories are the acceptance tests for the design. Each must be able to happen naturally, without scripting, in a normal playthrough. Playtesters should report which ones they experienced.

### Story 1: Your junk is their treasure

> I'm mining and having trouble finding materials. I come across a little cavern with creatures in it. I befriend them and give them stuff I've been gathering that wasn't useful to me, but they love it. They teach me something or show me a place full of stuff they don't care about but I love. It really sets me on my way.

- **Needs:** cultural gift values, colony placement next to disdained deposits, Places and Skills teachings, storyteller dry-streak nudge.
- **Passes when:** most new players reach a colony within 25 minutes, and a gift of a common material produces a teaching or map pin that noticeably speeds their progress.

### Story 2: Saved by a stranger, then even, then family

> I get crazy lucky and find amazing stuff, but I dig too deep, it gets too hot and I die. I wake up and someone has saved me. I've never seen them before and didn't know there were other creatures. Later I rescue them from danger, we're even, we become best friends, and they bring me to their family.

- **Needs:** heat that ramps with depth, nearest-healer rescue, wandering healers, waking at the rescuer's home, life debts, creature errands into danger, storyteller timing, Kin bond and family introduction.
- **Passes when:** a player who dies before meeting any creature always wakes with a stranger, and gets a chance to repay the debt within roughly 1–2 hours of play.

### Story 3: The flood I caused

> I dig for gold above a colony, breach an aquifer and flood their homes. They're upset. I build pumps and a wall to save them, and afterward they teach me glasswork.

- **Needs:** fluid simulation, colony memory of harm, building for colonies, bond repair, technique teachings.
- **Passes when:** player-caused floods can reach colonies, the colony reacts, and fixing it repairs and raises the bond.

### Story 4: The alloy nobody told me about

> I melt two metals together just to see what happens, and the result has a trait that makes my new pick glow and cut through stone twice as fast.

- **Needs:** alloying, seed-based bonus traits, trait effects on tools, codex updates.
- **Passes when:** each world has at least two alloy pairs with bonus traits, and they differ between seeds.

### Story 5: Two minutes on the bus

> I open the game, I'm right where I left off. I dig down, fill my pack, sell ore to my friends' colony for something I needed, and close the app. I made progress.

- **Needs:** instant resume, constant saving, short trips, nearby trading partners.
- **Passes when:** a full dig-trade trip fits in about 2–3 minutes once a player has a nearby colony, and nothing is lost when the app closes mid-trip.
