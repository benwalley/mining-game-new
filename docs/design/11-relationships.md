## 11. Relationships and kindness

Kindness is the most powerful strategy in the game. Giving away things that are useless to the player but precious to a creature should regularly change the course of a playthrough.

### Bonds

The player has a bond with each individual creature they've met. A colony's attitude is the average of its members' bonds, plus gossip from other colonies.

| Bond       | Reached by                                                | Unlocks                                                                         |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stranger   | Default                                                   | Wary icons; basic barter only                                                   |
| Acquainted | First friendly interaction or small gift                  | Fair trades; they share simple tips (an icon pointing to nearby ore)            |
| Friend     | Several gifts or one real help                            | A teaching; they visit your camp; better trade rates                            |
| Close      | Solving a problem for their colony, or many gifts         | Access to their colony station; bigger teachings; they may come along on a trip |
| Kin        | Repaying a life debt (section 12), or saving their colony | Family introductions; the whole colony starts at Friend; their best secrets     |

Bonds can drop. Flooding a colony, taking materials they treasure from their cavern, or collapsing their homes lowers bonds, and they remember who did it. Repair is always possible through help and gifts.

### Gifts

- **Value is cultural.** A gift's value is the species' cultural value for that material, not its usefulness to the player. Common dirt can be a treasure to the right species.
- **Surprise matters.** The first time a creature receives a treasured material, the reaction is big: a celebration animation, a song, the whole colony gathering.
- **Diminishing repeats.** Repeated gifts of the same thing to the same individual lose value. Variety and thoughtfulness are rewarded.
- **Crafted gifts (Should).** A crafted item made from a treasured material is worth more than the raw material. Wrapping it in a gift box adds a bonus for cultures that value presentation.

### Teachings

Teachings are the main reward for kindness. They come in four kinds, and every species offers some of each:

1. **Skills:** a lasting ability, such as sensing nearby ore as a faint shimmer, breathing longer in gas, or walking on hot ground.
2. **Techniques:** a crafting template or process (section 8).
3. **Places:** a map pin to a deposit or cavern they know about but don't care for. These are often exactly what the player has been searching for.
4. **Company:** a creature joins the player for a trip, lighting the way, warning of hazards or carrying extra load (Could).

### Gossip

When traders and scouts move between colonies, they carry news about the player. A colony that has heard good things greets the player at Acquainted instead of Stranger, and bad deeds travel the same way. This is what makes kindness compound across the world.

### Generator support for story 1

The world generator deliberately sets up the "your junk is their treasure" moment:

- Each colony's treasured materials include at least one that is common in the layers above it, so the player is likely carrying some on arrival.
- Each colony sits near a deposit it disdains but the player needs at that depth. Its "Places" teaching points there.
- The first colony the player is likely to reach is guaranteed to have a Places or Skills teaching available at Friend.
