## 18. Build roadmap

Build the game in seven phases, proving the feel first and layering systems on top. Don't start a phase until the previous one's gate passes in a playtest.

&#91;embedded content: build roadmap · 7 phases, each with a playtest gate\]

> **Added during the build:** the roadmap is an image on page 31 of the PDF and didn't make
> it into the Markdown export. Its phases are:
>
> 0. **Feel**
> 1. **Living rock:** world gen, fluids, heat, gas, cave-ins, light, zones, saving
> 2. **Hands:** crafting and building
> 3. **Neighbors:** one species, bonds, rescue
> 4. **Many peoples**
> 5. **A world of its own:** traits, alloys, journal, storyteller
> 6. **Polish:** audio, art, PWA, export
>
> The owner has since changed this plan. Cave-ins were dropped entirely, and some phase 1
> work moved to phase 2. [`docs/status.md`](../status.md) has the plan as it stands now.

Phases 0 and 1 are the riskiest technically, and phase 3 is the first time the game's heart is playable. Keep content small until phase 3 passes; more materials and species are cheap to add later because everything is data.
