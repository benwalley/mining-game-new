## 13. Goals without a quest

There is no required goal, ending or score. Instead the game offers three layers of things to try for, and the player picks what they care about.

### Journal

The journal is the player's record of everything learned in this world. Filling it is a goal in itself.

- **Codex:** every material found, with properties revealed as the player discovers them through use. Unknown properties show as "?".
- **Bestiary:** every species and critter met: culture, treasures, tradition, home layer.
- **People:** every individual the player knows, their bond, and any life debts.
- **Techniques:** templates and processes learned, and who taught them.
- **Map:** explored areas, markers, colonies and pins from teachings.
- **Ambitions:** see below.

### Ambitions

Each world generates 5–8 big optional ambitions from its traits and species. They appear in the journal once the player learns about them, often from creatures. Examples:

- Reach the Deep and learn what lies there.
- Open the sealed city (needs techniques from several species).
- Connect every colony by rail.
- Stop the fungal bloom before it reaches the Stone layer.
- Become Kin with every species.
- Forge a tool from the world's unique mineral.

Ambitions have no timers. Completing one triggers a celebration and changes the world in a lasting way (a colony festival, a new trade route, a new area opening up). The world keeps going afterward.

### Milestones

Small moments are recognized automatically with a short, quiet toast: first furnace, first friend, first alloy, first rescue, a new layer reached, a colony saved. They give short sessions a steady trickle of progress. No rewards attached; the recognition is the point.
