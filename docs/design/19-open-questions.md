## 19. Open questions and assumptions

These were not decided by the owner. Items marked _assumed_ are written into this doc as the default; confirm them before building the affected system.

- [ ] **Swipe style** (_assumed: hold-to-move_). Is movement swipe-and-hold that continues until the finger lifts, or a quick flick per tile?
- [ ] **Combat.** Can the player fight predators, or only avoid, wall off and outsmart them?
- [ ] **Death cost** (_assumed: keep your pack_). Should dying drop some or all of the pack at the spot where you fell, to be recovered?
- [ ] **Permanent creature deaths.** Can named creatures, including friends, die for good from simulation events?
- [ ] **Orientation** (_assumed: portrait first_). Is landscape needed at launch?
- [ ] **Art direction** (_assumed: 16×16 pixel art, \~32-color palette_). Any reference games or mood boards?
- [ ] **The surface.** Is there anything to do above ground beyond the starting camp: day and night, weather, trading posts?
- [ ] **Name.** "Deepkind" is a placeholder.
- [ ] **Target devices.** Which phones and browsers are the minimum to support?
- [ ] **Distribution.** Hosted website, installable web app only, or wrapped later as a store app?
- [ ] **Players' languages.** English only at first, or plan for translation? (Icon-based creature speech already helps.)

> **Decided since (owner, 2026-10-28):**
>
> - **Swipe style:** hold-to-move. The direction from the touch-down point to the finger sets the move vector.
> - **Target devices:** iOS Safari 17+ and Chrome on Android 2023+.
>
> Everything else here is still open. Ask before building a system that depends on it.
