## 15. The storyteller

A light, invisible director watches the player's recent experience and tilts the odds so good stories happen more often. It never hands out rewards directly and never breaks the simulation's rules.

### What it watches

- Time since the last meaningful discovery (new material, cavern, colony, teaching).
- Recent danger: near-deaths and deaths in the last stretch of play.
- Open life debts and how long they've been open.
- Whether the player is stuck: low tool power for the current depth, low materials, no new knowledge.

### What it can do

| Situation                       | Nudge                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Long dry streak while digging   | Bias the next not-yet-generated chunks toward a cavern, a colony edge or a richer vein |
| Player stuck on tool power      | Make a creature with a relevant teaching more likely to approach the player            |
| Open life debt                  | Time the rescuer's next risky errand to happen near the player                         |
| Several dangers in a row        | Hold back new hazards for a while; let the player recover                              |
| Colony problem ignored for long | Let the problem stabilize instead of escalating into disaster                          |

### Rules it must follow

- **Only unseen space.** It may only influence chunks that haven't been generated or seen yet, and creature decisions that happen out of sight.
- **Never invent.** It biases choices the simulation would already make. It never spawns items or creatures from nothing.
- **Bounded.** Every nudge has a cap and a cooldown, so luck still feels like luck.
- **Respect world traits.** It never undoes a trait. A hot world stays hot.
